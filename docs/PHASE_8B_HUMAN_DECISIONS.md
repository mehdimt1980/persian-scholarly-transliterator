# Phase 8B human scholarly decisions

The following remain visible rather than mechanically “corrected”:

- Unwritten or disputed Persian short vowels. A model-supplied `e/o` can produce `IJMES_PERSIAN_SHORT_VOWEL_SUSPECTED`, but the software does not globally substitute `i/u`.
- Proper-name, place-name, and IJMES Word List identities without scoped reviewed evidence. Mechanical diacritic removal does not establish an accepted English spelling.
- Proposed izafat and disputed post-vocalic `-yi`. Presentation preserves supplied structure and does not create a relation.
- Lexical substitutions, borrowed forms, unknown compound semantics, and morphology not established by existing evidence.
- Ambiguous official guidance or rules outside the bounded matrix in `PHASE_8B_POLICY_AUDIT.md`.

`BLOCK` is reserved for deterministic contradictions or unsupported canonical characters in the scoped checks. `REVIEW_REQUIRED` records a plausible scholarly concern with insufficient authority for automatic correction. `INFO` records a relevant boundary or source-policy note. Human acceptance preserves AI provenance and is not promotion into the reviewed lexicon or frozen authority.
